# FLOORPIT ($FLOORPIT)

Launch a coin. Open the pit.

**Live site:** https://useliving.github.io/floorpit/  
**X:** https://x.com/UseFloorPit

## How it works
Every coin launched on FLOORPIT opens with its own trading pit. The creator writes the charter: how orders match (order book, sealed bids, call auction or hybrid), which markets list, where liquidity comes from and what the pit charges. A share of realized net revenue can buy back the coin.

## What's on the site
- A live pixel-art trading pit: click the floor to shout bids and offers, switch matching models, add market makers
- Candlestick board, order ladder and live pit stats, plus market buy/sell, buyback and reset controls
- Charter builder with live fee and buyback estimates and a demo wallet
- The floor: sample pits with live charts and totals
- Flywheel, comparison, revenue lines, launch strategy and the story behind the name
- Pixel ticker tape background that reacts to your cursor

Single self-contained `index.html`.

> Pre-launch: every pit, trade and number on the site is simulated. FLOORPIT is not a regulated exchange, and buybacks are never guaranteed. Memecoins are extremely risky. Not financial advice.
