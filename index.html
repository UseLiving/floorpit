Why your own pit

Your own venue, not a listing.

A coin listed on someone else's exchange

  • ×You don't set the fees, the rules or how orders match.
  • ×Volume you bring earns money for the venue, not for you.
  • ×Your market sits next to thousands of others.
  • ×No say in which pairs trade or who makes the market.

A coin with its own pit on FLOORPIT

  • ✓You own the venue: matching model, pairs and fee schedule.
  • ✓Execution fees come back to the pit and can buy back the coin.
  • ✓Bring your own makers, agents or existing DEX liquidity.
  • ✓Change the charter as the market grows.

About the name

Why FLOORPIT?

The old exchanges ran on a trading floor: a room full of pits where traders shouted bids and offers until someone took the other side. Every price was made out loud, in public.

FLOORPIT brings that room to memecoins. Every coin gets a pit of its own on the floor, you write the rules it trades by, and the fees it earns can buy it back and help hold up the other kind of floor: the price.

The fine print

FLOORPIT is not a regulated exchange, trading venue or broker, and opening a pit here does not make it one. Calling something a pit or a venue is a description, not a legal status.

Buybacks depend on realized revenue and are never guaranteed. This page is a pre-launch preview: every pit, trade and number on it is simulated. Memecoins are extremely risky. Nothing here is financial advice.
